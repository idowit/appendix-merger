# Separate Appendix Compilation from Quick Merge

Make Appendix Compilation the primary workspace and expose Quick Merge as a secondary workflow. Appendix Compilation requires one Main Document and at least one Appendix so its TOC, covers, titles, numbering, and markers remain coherent; Quick Merge accepts an unrestricted ordered set and concatenates it without appendix semantics. This separation preserves a fast general merge utility without weakening the product's lawyer-focused domain language or complicating its main workflow.
